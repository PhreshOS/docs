---
title: Text fields
description: Labeled single-line and multi-line text controls.
---

`Input` is a labeled single-line text control. `Textarea` is the multi-line
form. `onChange` receives the string value. Both share label, description,
error message, disabled, read-only, required, invalid, size, color, radius,
material, and shadow.

<TextFieldShowcase />

The field owns its label and feedback. The inner Surface owns paint. Focus,
invalid, and hover states change the Surface paint through the shared control
theme.
