---
title: Panel
description: Compose an outer Surface, optional header, and inset content Surface.
---

`Panel` owns one Surface composition: an outer material, an optional header,
and an inset content material.

```text
Surface
├── header
└── Surface
    └── content
```

<PanelShowcase />

The outer Surface receives the ordinary Panel and Surface props.
`Panel.Header` owns the optional leading region. `Panel.Content` is the
constrained inner Surface and receives its own native and Surface properties.
The two Surfaces remain independently configurable.
